Ink

Press and hold the paper to pour.

Tilt your phone, and wet ink runs with it.

Switch the tool to the brush to sweep: slow is thick and wet, fast is thin and dry, and the brush runs out of ink as it goes.

One of you splashes, the other outlines: reading the ink and drawing a form out of it with a fine brush. Wet paper looks darker; outline it while wet and the line blooms, wait and it stays crisp.

The more you pour, the paler the ink. Pause, and it darkens again.

The paper dries. What falls cannot be taken back.

When you are ready, pass the paper. Your partner answers only in ink, in its own role.

The Friction button turns each of these resistances up or down.

Your strokes are recorded in this browser so you can replay them. Nothing leaves your device unless you export it, or pass the paper to a partner that runs elsewhere.
